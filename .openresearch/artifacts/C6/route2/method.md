# C6 route 2 method

An executable schema checker compares the paper text against the fields needed to
instantiate one Fashion-MNIST DDPM. Its negative control fills every missing field
with explicit example values and must pass, demonstrating that the checker does not
reject every protocol.

On HF `cpu-upgrade`, a deliberately tiny three-convolution network is benchmarked
with eight Torch threads. It has no U-Net hierarchy, residual blocks, normalization,
time MLP, dropout, or EMA, so its measured throughput is an optimistic lower bound
on compute time rather than a stand-in model.
