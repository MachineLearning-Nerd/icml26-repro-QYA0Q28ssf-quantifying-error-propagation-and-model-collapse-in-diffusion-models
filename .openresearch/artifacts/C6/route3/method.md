# C6 route 3 method

An executable source-contract checker enumerates the fields required to instantiate
the CIFAR-10 model, training recursion, feature-space drift metric, and observability
estimator. It separately enumerates direct evidence needed for the alpha-tradeoff.

The non-vacuous control fills every required protocol field with explicit mock
values and must pass. The checker also requires the paper's actual positive CIFAR
evidence—the observability figure—to remain acknowledged.
