# C4–C5 limitations and deviations

- C4's finite sequences calibrate the exact proof recursion; the proof certificate,
  not the sequences, carries the universal result.
- C5 is falsified only as written with standard multiplicative `asymp` and
  `C_bias>0`.
- The falsification does not challenge the appendix's weaker additive inequalities.
- A corrected theorem with `C_bias` on both sides, `C_bias=0`, or additive-error
  notation would require a new claim and proof.
