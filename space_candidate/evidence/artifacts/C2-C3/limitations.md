# C2–C3 limitations and deviations

- The controlled score error is deterministic in time rather than learned from data.
- The non-Gaussian mixture reaches a maximum exact observability below one; the
  separate analytic Gaussian control covers the endpoint `eta=1`.
- Configuration checks are not treated as proof of a universal theorem.
- The paper does not publish executable proof certificates or raw Figure 3/4 data;
  this is an independent reconstruction from the hashed v2 source.
