# C6 route 4 method

The independent checker receives candidate records containing an explicit
assumption audit, an observable-identity check, and a contradiction test. It
computes strict ordering and threshold contradictions itself.

Four defensible candidates are checked: the paper-scale population-KDE route, its
h=0 mechanism control, the positive alpha=0.9 drift value, and overlapping CIFAR
observability. A synthetic claim with an explicit 10% upper threshold must be
falsified by the same checker; this proves the gate can accept a contradiction when
the contract actually defines one. The synthetic threshold is never attributed to
the paper.
