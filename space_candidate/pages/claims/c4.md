# C4 — Proposition 4.1

Current pre-publication status: **exact proof implemented; formal run pending**.

The source has two separate conclusions:

1. `sum eps_i^2=infinity` implies `sum D_i=infinity`.
2. An eventual pointwise floor `eps_i^2>=eps_floor>0` additionally implies
   `limsup D_i>=alpha*eta_floor*eps_floor/(16*(1+(1-alpha)^2))`.

The first clause alone does **not** imply a nonzero floor. The current verifier
therefore includes a control with divergent `eps_i^2=1/(i+1)` and a recurrence-valid
`D_i` that tends to zero while remaining non-summable. Removing the uniform
per-generation floor from the second clause makes the proof checker reject.

This supersedes the historical scalar recursion, which did not test the paper's
specific constant or distinguish the two quantifiers.
