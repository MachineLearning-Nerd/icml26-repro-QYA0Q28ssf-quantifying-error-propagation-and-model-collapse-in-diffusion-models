# C5 — Theorem 4.2: FALSIFIED as written

## Exact contract

The source defines `f asymp g` by fixed finite positive multiplicative constants.
Under A1–A5, positive observability, perturbative errors, and
`sum eps_star_i²<infinity`, Theorem 4.2 (`#S4.Thmtheorem2`) states that a fixed
`C_bias>0` exists and

`D_(N+1)+C_bias asymp discounted_energy_N + decayed_initial_N`.

For every alpha>0, summability makes the entire right side tend to zero. Since
chi-squared divergence is nonnegative, the left side is at least `C_bias>0`.
Therefore no fixed finite upper multiplicative constant can satisfy the displayed
equivalence.

## Machine-checkable contradiction

The certificate checks the exact source definition, positivity of the bias,
vanishing of both right-hand terms, and nonnegativity of `D_N`. **Assumption
audit:** A1–A5, positive observability, perturbative errors, and summability are
enumerated in the contract; the contradiction uses the last three source
conditions and divergence nonnegativity.

| alpha=0.1 horizon | minimum upper asymp constant required |

The required constant diverges. The appendix proves weaker additive inequalities,
