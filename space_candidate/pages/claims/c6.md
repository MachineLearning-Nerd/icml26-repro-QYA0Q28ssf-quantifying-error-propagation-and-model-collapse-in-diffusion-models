# C6 — empirical alpha tradeoff

Current status: **BLOCKED; route 1 formal run pending**.

The exact paper protocol combines three substantial targets: a 100,000-sample 10D
KDE experiment over 20 generations and 10 seeds, a 200-epoch plus recursive
Fashion-MNIST DDPM campaign, and a CIFAR-10 campaign. The paper publishes no code,
checkpoints, raw numbers, U-Net widths, batch size, DDPM beta schedule, or finite-KDE
acceleration rule.

Route 1 tests the population limit of the paper's GMM KDE at its stated dimension,
geometry, N, generations, alpha values, and run count. It predicts exact
within-component variance

`sigma^2 + h^2*(1-(1-alpha)^n)/alpha`,

and uses 100,000 stochastic samples per alpha/seed/checkpoint for confidence
intervals. An `h=0` control must remove all alpha-dependent accumulation.

Even if aligned, this route cannot verify full C6: finite empirical KDE, the 500-step
solver, Fashion-MNIST, and CIFAR-10 remain unresolved. Current verification is placed
first; no historical toy result is presented as the C6 verifier.
